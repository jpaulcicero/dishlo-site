Questions

Fair questions,
straight answers.

Where can I get Dishlo?

Dishlo is live on Google Play for Android. An iPhone version is planned — email us and we'll let you know when it arrives.

How do I get recipes into Dishlo?

Four ways: snap a photo of a cookbook page, add a screenshot from your camera roll, paste recipe text, or upload a PDF. Dishlo reads it, extracts the ingredients and instructions, and puts the extracted text in front of you to review before you save. You can edit the recipe afterward.

Can Dishlo import a recipe from a link?

No — and that's deliberate. Dishlo doesn't scrape websites or fetch content from URLs. Recipes belong to the people who wrote them, so you add the content yourself from sources you have access to. The URL field exists to credit and remember where a recipe came from.

How does the grocery list actually work?

You pick a date range and Dishlo gathers every ingredient from every meal planned in that range, combines duplicates, converts units so amounts add up properly, and sorts everything by store category. Pantry Check lets you hide things you already have before you head out.

Does it work offline?

Dishlo is built to keep working on a weak connection. Plan and grocery-list changes save on your device first, then sync automatically when you're back online. Signing in and adding a new recipe do need an internet connection.

What happens to my data?

It stays yours. No ads, no selling data, and your content is private to your account. Read the plain-English details in our Privacy Policy.

How much does it cost, and how do I cancel?

Dishlo is $6.99 a month after a 14-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Cancel any time in your Google Play subscription settings — if you cancel before the trial ends, you pay nothing. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes, any time — in the app under Account, or by email. See how to delete your account.
